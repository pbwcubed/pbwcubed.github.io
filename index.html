I am a PhD student at Humboldt University Berlin under the supervision of Bruno Klingler. Previously, I was an M.Sc student at Freie University Berlin and a B.Sc. student at the Technical University Berlin and the University of Vienna.

My doctoral thesis is about local systems and non-abelian Hodge theory, but I am generally interested in algebraic geometry and number theory.

I am funded by the ERC Grant TameHodge. Moreover, I am a member of the Berlin Mathematical School, and an associate member of the Berlin-Hanover RTG 2965 - From Geometry to Numbers: Moduli, Hodge Theory, Rational Points.

I am on the job market for postdoctoral positions starting in summer/ fall 2027.
